The honest guide

Which service is right for your car?

Just need it clean for the week?

An Express Wash tier covers it.

Interior's gotten away from you? Kids, pets, crumbs?

Start with the Signature Interior Detail, and add Carpet Extraction or Dog Hair Removal if needed.

Paint looking dull or swirled?

Add a Clay Bar and Buff Wax to your exterior detail, or ask about Ceramic Wax for longer-lasting shine and easier upkeep.

Selling the car, hosting guests, or it just needs everything?

The Platinum Detail is our best work, top to bottom, in one day.

Cloudy headlights?

Headlight Restoration is a 40-minute fix that also improves night visibility.

Not sure which fits? Get a quote first and tell us what you drive. We'll point you to the right service instead of the most expensive one.
