How it works

How our fleet detailing service works.

  1. Tell us what you're working with

    Number of vehicles, vehicle types (sedans, trucks, vans), and how often you need service: weekly, biweekly, monthly, or as-needed.

  2. We build a schedule and a rate

    Fleet pricing is based on volume and vehicle mix, using our Express Wash tiers and Signature Detail packages as the base menu.

  3. You drop off or block-book

    Vehicles rotate through on the schedule we set, with flexible timing so your team isn't waiting around.

  4. One invoice, not ten receipts

    Fleet accounts get consolidated billing so your books stay simple, and regular maintenance detailing keeps every vehicle on a predictable schedule instead of an emergency one.
