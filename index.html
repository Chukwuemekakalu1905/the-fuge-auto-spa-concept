09 · Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

What's the difference between a wash and a full detail?

A wash is the quick, walk-in service: four express tiers from $11 to $22, done while you wait. A detail is a scheduled deep-clean: hours of hands-on work on the interior, exterior, or both, restoring the car rather than just cleaning it.

Do I need an appointment?

Not for a wash. Just drive up any time: Mon–Sat 7:30 AM to 7:00 PM, or Sunday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Detailing is by appointment so we can give your car the time it deserves; book online through Booksy, send the form above, or give us a call.

How much does a detail cost?

Signature details run $150 for interior on small cars and sedans ($225 for oversize interiors like large trucks, minivans, and large SUVs), $200 for exterior, and $400 for both. The full-day Platinum detail is $800 for a sedan or wagon and $995 for oversize vehicles, and add-ons like headlight restoration ($80) or ceramic wax ($150) are available a la carte.

Can I really work or hang out while I wait?

That's the whole idea. The lounge has ten remote-work stations with high-speed internet, power at every seat, free WiFi, and natural light, plus a putting green that's free for wash and detail customers.

Are the food trucks there every weekend?

A rotating lineup of local food trucks parks at The Fuge Friday through Sunday, 12:00 to 7:00 PM. The truck changes, so follow @fugeautospa on Instagram to see who is up next.

Do you have something for kids?

Yes. We have a kids' corner with a big chalkboard to draw on and a Little Free Library stocked with free children's books they can take home.

Do you do headlight restoration?

We do. Cloudy, yellowed lenses are polished back to clear in about 40 minutes for $80, for better looks and noticeably better night visibility. It books like a detail: online, through the form, or by phone.

Are you part of a chain?

No. The Fuge is independent and community-built, right here in Gilbert. We're the only true full-service wash available within five miles, and part of our mission is raising money for local clubs, teams, churches, and charities.
