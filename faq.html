FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

What's the difference between a wash and a full detail?

A wash is the quick, walk-in service: four express tiers from $11 to $22, done while you wait. A detail is a scheduled deep-clean: hours of hands-on work on the interior, exterior, or both, restoring the car rather than just cleaning it.

Do I need an appointment?

Not for a wash. Just drive up any time: Mon–Sat 7:30 AM to 7:00 PM, or Sunday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Detailing is by appointment so we can give your car the time it deserves; book online through Booksy, send the form, or give us a call.

How much does a detail cost?

Signature details run $150 for interior on small cars and sedans ($225 for oversize interiors), $200 for exterior, and $400 for the combined interior & exterior detail. Platinum is $800 for sedans/wagons and $995 oversize. Add-ons are priced on the detailing page.

Do you offer memberships?

Yes — Unlimited wash memberships run $21–$95/mo depending on tier. See memberships for the concept tiers.
