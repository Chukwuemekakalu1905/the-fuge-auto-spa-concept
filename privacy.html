Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Fuge Auto Spa ("we," "us," or "our"), located at 615 West Ray Road, Suite A, Gilbert, AZ 85233, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us:

Contact and quote requests. When you use our contact form, it opens your own email app with the details you entered — such as your name, phone number, email address, vehicle information, and message — and nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. Nothing is stored by this website.

Direct communications. If you call or email us, we receive the information you share in that conversation.

We do not require accounts. Our hosting provider may collect standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the website.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party trackers, fonts, or embeds. There is nothing to accept or decline.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and quote requests
  • To schedule and provide wash and detailing services
  • To communicate about appointments, pricing, and community programs

Third-party services

Parts of our website link to third-party services that have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review:

  • Booksy handles online booking; anything you enter there is governed by Booksy's terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Maps provides directions when you follow a directions link.
  • Instagram and Facebook host our social profiles, which we link to.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above as needed to operate the website and our business, or when required by law.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to serve you and run our business, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request a correction or deletion, by emailing thefugeautospa@gmail.com or calling 480-299-5771.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision, and changes take effect when posted on this page.
