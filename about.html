About The Fuge

The Fuge is short for refuge.

An independent car wash and detail center in Gilbert, built by neighbors, for neighbors, around one idea: the errand you dread at other washes should be the best stop of your day here.

Why we exist

Most washes want you gone. We want you comfortable.

The modern car wash is engineered to move you through it: three minutes in a tunnel, a vending machine, a plastic chair if you're lucky. We think Gilbert deserves better, so we built the opposite.

At The Fuge, a wash comes with a place to actually be: a bright lounge with ten work stations and free WiFi, a putting green, a kids corner with a chalkboard and free books, and food trucks on the weekends. And detailing here is a craft, done by hand, by the same small crew, at posted prices with honest durations.

We're also the only true full-service wash available within five miles, which we take as a responsibility: to be the neighborhood spot worth keeping.

What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

Independent, on purpose

We're not a chain and not a franchise. Decisions get made here in Gilbert by the people who will shake your hand at the counter, and it shows in the work.

Attention over throughput

Big washes are built to move cars. The Fuge is built to care for them: real hands, real hours, and the same standard whether you drive a work truck or a weekend car.

The wait should be a gift

A refuge is a place you want to be. Natural light, room to work, a putting green, and a corner for the kids: we built the wait we wished existed.

Profit with a purpose

Our goal is to raise $250,000 a year for local clubs, teams, churches, and charities through co-sponsored wash days. Clean cars, funding good things.

Community

Every wash gives something back.

Local clubs, teams, churches, and charities can co-sponsor a wash day, with a share of that day's washes going straight to their cause. We've already hosted days like a dog-adoption fundraiser with Rotten Rottie Rescue, and we're just getting started.

Partner with us

Ready when you are

Your car gets the spa. You get the refuge.
